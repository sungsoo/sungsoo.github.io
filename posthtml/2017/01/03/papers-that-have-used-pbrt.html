    Papers that have used pbrt

    According to Google Scholar, the Physically Based Rendering book has been cited by over 1200 research papers. Here we have acccumulated a list of all of the research papers that we are aware of that have used the pbrt software in some way as a basis for their work.

    Please send us a note at authors@pbrt.org if you have used pbrt in a published research paper so that we can add your paper to this list.
